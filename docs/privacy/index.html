Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

The short version. This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or sign-up forms, and we do not collect personal information through it. The NDA Handshake software runs on your own computer and sends nothing to us.

1. Who we are

This website, ultravireslabs.com, is operated by Ultra Vires Labs LLC, a New York limited liability company ("Ultra Vires Labs", "we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you visit the website or use the software we publish.

2. What this website collects

We do not ask for or collect personal information through this website. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels. Its pages do not load content from third parties.

3. Our hosting provider

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information when your browser requests a page, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and may keep it in logs for security and operational purposes. We do not have access to those logs. GitHub's handling of that information is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

4. Downloads

Download links on this website point to files hosted by GitHub. When you download a file, GitHub handles the request under its own privacy statement. We see only the public download counts that GitHub reports for a project.

5. The NDA Handshake software

NDA Handshake runs entirely on your own computer.

  • Your company's NDA positions, its confirmation and its handshake records are stored in files on your computer, in a location you choose.
  • The software does not send any information to Ultra Vires Labs. It has no telemetry, no usage reporting and no account system.
  • Handshake emails travel between you and the other company through your own email account. We are not in the middle of that exchange and never see it.
  • The AI assistant you use with the software is provided by someone else, and what you share with it is subject to that provider's terms and privacy policy.

6. When you contact us or deal with us directly

If you contact us, or if your company does an NDA handshake with Ultra Vires Labs itself as the other party, we receive what you send, such as names, email addresses, your company's NDA positions and the resulting agreement. We use that information only to respond to you and to make, keep and perform the agreement, and we keep it for as long as we need it for those purposes or as the law requires. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

7. Public places

Our projects are developed in public repositories on GitHub. Anything you post there, such as an issue or a comment, is public and is handled by GitHub under its own policies.

8. Other websites

This website links to other sites, including attorneysatclaw.xyz and GitHub. Each has its own privacy practices, and this policy does not cover them.

9. Children

This website and our software are intended for businesses and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect personal information through this website, there is usually nothing for us to provide, correct or delete. If you have dealt with us directly and want to know what we hold about you, or want it corrected or deleted, contact us and we will respond as the law requires.

11. Changes

If our practices change, we will update this policy here and change the effective date above.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's issue tracker on GitHub. Please do not post anything confidential or personal there, because it is public.
